# Legacy EPH preprocessing inventory

## Scope and evidence

This inventory records the former producer; it is not an operating guide. The executable producer is `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py`; `src/encuestador/preprocess.py` is a later, incomplete extraction whose CPI block is explicitly a hook. The notebooks preserve earlier exploration, while `recalcular_rankings.py` only republishes rank columns already embedded in training CSVs. Artifact-level metadata is in [`legacy_artifact_inventory.csv`](legacy_artifact_inventory.csv).

## Producer graph

1. **Sources.** For each inclusive year range (`-y`, default 2021–2024), the producer requests up to four semicolon-delimited household and individual files from `microdatos-EPH-INDEC` into `./temp_data`. `--overwrite` changes skip-existing behavior. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:10-24,68-132`.
2. **Geography.** `data/info/radio_ref.csv` supplies the distinct `AGLOMERADO`→`Region` lookup. Ambiguous Pampeana rows for agglomerates 33 and 93 are manually removed, assigning their remaining GBA/Patagonia values. An inner merge with no explicit keys uses all common columns (effectively `AGLOMERADO`). Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:26-34,205-207`.
3. **Households.** Read 19 named columns; concatenate quarters, remove exact duplicates, exclude `IV1=9`, collapse `IV10`, `II9`, and `II7` categories, and cap `IX_TOT` at 8. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:136-151`.
4. **Persons and schema patch.** Read a tolerant allow-list, rename `ESTADO`→`CONDACT`; if aggregate `V2_M`/`V5_M` is absent, sum all three corresponding component columns, otherwise use zero when the complete trio is absent. This rule entered in commit `59b7533` for the 2025 schema. Rows without `P47T` and exact duplicates are removed. `CH15`, `CH09`, `CH12`/`CH13` are census-aligned, negative age is clipped to zero, and children under 14 get `CONDACT=0`. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:158-198`; `git show 59b7533`.
5. **Entity merge.** Household and person frames inner-join on `CODUSU, ANO4, TRIMESTRE, AGLOMERADO`. There is no person sequence key in the selected contract, so the output is person-level but stable person uniqueness cannot be asserted. Shared non-key fields are removed from the individual side first. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:199-203`.
6. **Synthetic pooled sample.** A seeded, with-replacement sample of `int(0.05*n)` person rows is appended with `AGLOMERADO=0`. A stale comment says 10%; executable behavior is 5%. These are training/sample rows, not survey observations, and retain `CODUSU`. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:209-216`.
7. **Monetary normalization.** Quarter dates are the 15th of the middle month. Nine monetary fields are multiplied by the January-2016 monthly IPC index and divided by the quarter-date `index` from `indice_precios_Q.csv`, then rounded. Thus units are nominal ARS expressed at the January-2016 reference, subject to the external `IPC-Argentina` series vintage. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:38-59,222-230`; README methodology.
8. **Census naming and exclusions.** Twenty-four EPH household/person columns are renamed to census-style fields. Then `P47T < -0.001` is excluded, all remaining missing values become zero, and code 9 is excluded for seven named categorical variables. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:36-43,233-240`.
9. **Ranks and targets.** Within each year, mean normalized `P47T` among `CAT_OCUP==3` and `P47T>=100` is percentile-ranked by agglomerate and region, rounded to three decimals, then inner-merged. Four binary targets use `>100`. Output is sorted only by `CODUSU` and written to `data/training/EPHARG_train_YY.csv`. Evidence: `src/encuestador/preprocesar_datos.py:242-266`.
10. **Rank exports.** `recalcular_rankings.py` copies distinct embedded ranks for hard-coded 2023–2024 and adds normalized region slugs; `preprocess.export_ranks` instead recomputes them and accepts years. Outputs are `data/info/AGLO_rk.csv` and `Reg_rk.csv`. Evidence: `src/encuestador/recalcular_rankings.py:13-72`; `src/encuestador/preprocess.py:18-48`.
11. **Models/downstream.** `entrenar_modelos.py` reads annual training CSVs and stages three multi-output random-forest classifiers plus quarterly multi-output income regressors after `log10(clip(-0.9)+1)`. Random train/test splitting has no seed; regressors have one tree. Serialized `clf*` are downstream legacy artifacts. Evidence: `src/encuestador/entrenar_modelos.py:24-117`.

## Dependencies, automation, and reproducibility risks

- Direct data dependencies are `microdatos-EPH-INDEC`, `IPC-Argentina`, and local `radio_ref.csv`. Census projections, `empleoARG`, and department/region tables occur in the broader census/notebook estate but are not inputs to the annual CSV producer. The extracted `preprocess.py` additionally imports unavailable `eph_align`, described as an external aligner.
- The scheduled workflow ran every 21 days (despite a comment saying 18), accepted repository dispatches, invoked obsolete `./codigo/routines/...` paths, committed sub-50 MB data, then triggered ranking/model workflows that also use obsolete paths. These workflows are historical and must remain disabled. Evidence: `.github/workflows/workflow_preprocesar_datos.yml`; `.github/workflows/workflow_actualizar_modelos.yml`; `.github/workflows/workflow_recalcular_rankings.yml`.
- No survey weights are selected; no explicit duplicate-key assertion exists; `fillna(0)` conflates missing with category/value zero; implicit geography merges and external mutable URLs prevent exact reproduction without pinned inputs; `overwrite` and year handling differ across scripts. These behaviors should be regression cases, not silently endorsed methodology.

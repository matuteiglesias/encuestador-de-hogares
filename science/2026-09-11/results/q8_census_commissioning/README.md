# Q8 Census research commissioning

RESEARCH COMMISSIONING — NOT AN OFFICIAL POVERTY ESTIMATE.

This bundle applies the EPH-trained P1-R hurdle-Gamma mean model and EPH-only cross-fitted residual ECDF to the Census P1-R plane. Census has no outcome authority here. The Census P1 artifact contains no governed private-versus-collective dwelling indicator, so no unsupported universe split was invented.

A large transport shift is present: 28.8% of Census persons are weak-support under the predetermined marginal-support rule; the P1-R EPH-vs-Census domain classifier has in-sample AUC 0.874. Results are therefore commissioned with material transport caveats.
